What this measures

Citation accuracy measures the fraction of positive answered queries with at least one valid citation to a ground-truth evidence page, using local extractive answers. It does not measure full answer factuality. Results depend on the original seeded corpus and current permissions; modified or missing sources can make this benchmark fail.
